Okay. Let's see how we can use a four action framework for creating a new value proposition or a new business model or improving an existing one. The four action framework has been proposed by Chan Kim and Renee Montborg in their work, Blue Ocean Strategy. In this framework, we have four kinds of action. The first one is create. You can create new features of your value proposition or new components of your business model that the industry has never created before. The second kind of action is raise. You can raise some existing features or some existing component above the industry standards. But stronger. The third kind of actions are you can eliminate features or you can eliminate components of your business model. Eliminate factors that the industry has taken for granted before. And finally, the last kind of action is you can reduce some features or you can reduce some component below the industry standards. This is the four action framework that we will illustrate with the we business model.
